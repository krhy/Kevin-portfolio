✨ What's new
❌ Who still owes & 🔓 stay unlocked (November 2026)
  • At the top of Contributions, a bar shows how many people still owe and the total, e.g. “❌ 3 people still owe · $30”. Tap it to see each person, how many games and which dates.
  • It comes from the Reclub lists: when someone pays, the committee taps the game and sets them to ✅ Paid, and they drop off the list.
  • After entering the committee password, this phone stays unlocked for 7 days. Tap Lock to end it sooner. Changing the password signs out every phone.
👥 Reclub attendance (November 2026)
Each game can now show who paid, who has the fee waived and who hasn't paid yet, read straight from a Reclub screenshot.

For everyone

  • On the Contributions tab, each game shows a summary like “✅ 9 paid · 🟡 2 fee waived · ❌ 2 not paid · 🔒 1 reserved”.
  • Tap the summary to see the names in each group and the Reclub screenshot.

For the committee (after Unlock Editing)

  • Tap a game to edit it (look for “✎ Tap to edit”).
  • In the edit form, scroll to 👥 Reclub attendance and tap 📷 Upload Reclub screenshot. For long lists, add 2–3 screenshots; duplicates are merged.
  • The app reads the Confirmed list on your phone: names, Paid / Fee waived / Not paid badges, reserved slots and the confirmed count. It fills in Players and checks the total (e.g. “✅ 14 of 14”).
  • Check the list and fix anything: change a status, edit a name, ＋ add or ✕ remove a person. Reserved names can be added later.
  • Made a mistake? ✕ removes a screenshot, 🗑 Clear attendance starts over, and Cancel undoes everything since you opened the form.
  • Save saves the game and attendance together. Removed screenshots are deleted then. Every change is in the Activity Log.

Good to know

  • “Fee waived” is a label only — it doesn't change the funds.
  • The first screenshot downloads a small reader (about 7 MB, once). Wi-Fi is best.
  • The reader may drop emojis or get capital letters wrong — just fix them before saving.
